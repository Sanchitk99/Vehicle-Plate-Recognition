    About AutoVision ANPR

    Complete technical overview of the two-stage deep learning perception pipeline.

    High-Level Architecture

    AutoVision ANPR employs an end-to-end two-stage neural pipeline optimized for Indian road traffic conditions:

    1. Stage 1 - Localization: Fine-tuned YOLO11n operates in FP16 mode to detect vehicles (cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks) and license plates simultaneously with sub-15ms latency.
    2. Stage 2 - Geometric Pre-Processing: Detected plate crops are enhanced with adaptive CLAHE (Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization) and aspect-ratio normalization. High-Security Registration Plate (HSRP) blue "IND" emblems and decorative dealer frames are removed to prevent character bleeding.
    3. Stage 3 - Neural Recognition: A dedicated 5-layer CNN + 2-layer Bidirectional LSTM + CTC recognizer evaluates the plate crop in ~4ms, providing exact character sequences.
    4. Stage 4 - Slot Syntax Validation: Outputs are validated against the Indian Ministry of Road Transport & Highways standard (State Code [2 letters] → RTO District [2 digits] → Series [1-3 letters] → Unique Serial [4 digits]).
    5. Stage 5 - Temporal Tracking (Video Mode): An IoU-based multi-frame tracker links detections across frames, eliminates transient glitches, and logs unique vehicles with time intervals and snapshot thumbnails.

    Model Metrics Glossary

    Precision

    Ratio of true positive detections over all positive predictions. High precision indicates minimal false alarms.

    Recall

    Ratio of detected objects over all ground truth objects. High recall signifies low miss rates in crowded scenes.

    mAP@0.50

    Mean Average Precision computed at an IoU threshold of 0.50. Represents rapid localization accuracy.

    mAP@0.50:0.95

    Strict Average Precision averaged across IoU thresholds from 0.50 to 0.95 in steps of 0.05.

    Latency (P50 / P95)

    End-to-end processing duration. P50 represents typical speed; P95 measures tail-end performance under high batch load.

    Throughput (FPS)

    Estimated frames processed per second, calculated as 1000 divided by total pipeline latency in milliseconds.
